Study halls and the ENG Data Working Group

Maintained by the analytics team · updated this week · open to everyone in the directorate
Bring your own dashboard or query. No prerequisites, no grades. The best sessions are the ones where an analyst solves another analyst's problem.

Ground rules

Work on real dashboards and real datasets, not toy examples. Questions are the agenda. Anything learned gets written down here so it outlives the session.
