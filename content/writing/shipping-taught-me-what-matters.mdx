When I released the VisiTime tour, the work included Unity development, historical assets, device setup, and operating a rental fleet. The release took longer and cost more than I expected. A small change in the app could mean another round of testing, revised instructions, and coordination with the people running the tour.

If I did it again, I’d put a short section of the tour in visitors’ hands sooner. I’d want to learn whether the stops made sense on the ground, whether people could see and hear the content, and how much work it took to get an iPad ready for the next visitor.

Coding assistants could help with some of the setup and test writing today. I’d still put early time into watching people try the tour and making the next release smaller.
