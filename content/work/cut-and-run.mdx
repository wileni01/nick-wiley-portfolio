## From one sloop to a fleet

You start with a small crew and a ship. Trade or raid to build up your fleet. Every voyage has a cost, from provisions to the crew’s share of the take. I’m building the campaign around decisions that carry over after you leave port, including how you pay the crew and whose flag you sail under.

## Who lets you back into port

A governor’s commission can make a raid legal under one flag and turn you into a wanted captain under another. I’m developing the Lantern Compact as a network of captains, pilots, and harbor keepers who remember which promises you kept. The project site has work-in-progress images and a signup for the first look.
