Gettysburg visitors have to piece together information from tour operators, local calendars, and National Park Service notices. I built a single site that puts tour options, event listings, and closure notices in one place.

I wrote the import jobs that normalize records from different sources and match duplicate listings by source ID, title, time, and venue. The site exposes source links and verification dates so visitors can check when a price or closure was last confirmed. The public site also gives users a direct link to the operator when they’re ready to book.
