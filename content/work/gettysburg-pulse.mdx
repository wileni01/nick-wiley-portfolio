## Finding a tour or planning a day

I built GettysburgTours.com to bring information from tour operators, local venues, and the National Park Service into one guide. The redesigned homepage leads with tour search and puts upcoming events and park notices within reach. Visitors can narrow the catalog by category, listed price, duration, or family suitability, then follow the link to the operator to book. Listings show their sources and when they were last checked.

## Fixing the event calendar

A change to the Majestic Theater’s website broke the calendar import and left visitors with incorrect showtimes and duplicate listings. I rewrote the import to check each production’s published showtimes before replacing entries. The cleanup restored 51 performances from the theater’s schedules and removed duplicate, sample, and unverified historical entries from the public calendar.

Repeated imports match existing events by source page and showtime. If a schedule can’t be parsed, the last published entries stay up and the job records the failure for follow-up. The public pages also share a cache so routine visits don’t have to query the database each time.

## How I used coding assistants

I used Codex to update the Majestic Theater parser after the venue changed its site and add regression tests for duplicate and incorrect showtimes. I checked the corrected listings against the venue’s published schedule before putting them back online.
