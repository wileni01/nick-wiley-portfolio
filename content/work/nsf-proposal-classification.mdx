I built a pipeline that turned scientific abstracts into SciBERT embeddings, reduced their dimensions with UMAP, and compared HDBSCAN and k-means clustering. I used Optuna to tune the pipeline and built Plotly views so staff could inspect topic keywords and representative proposals.

About 30% of the corpus was left for manual review in the selected run. That gave staff a way to look at interdisciplinary or ambiguous proposals before assigning them to a theme.
