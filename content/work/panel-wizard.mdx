Program staff used eight screens and spreadsheets to compare proposals, track ratings, and assemble review panels. I built a Streamlit application that brought the relevant records into one screen.

The tool used sentence embeddings and k-means to suggest groups of related proposals. Staff could see match scores, move proposals between groups, and make the final assignments. The combined workflow reduced panel preparation from weeks to hours on the process we supported.
