I standardized addresses, geocoded recipients and contractors, and built map layers that connected awards to project locations. I used ESRI tools for spatial analysis and Palantir for exploring links among contractors, subcontractors, and recipients.

I also helped define how analytical systems represented awards, organizations, and locations so investigators could move between a map and the underlying records.
