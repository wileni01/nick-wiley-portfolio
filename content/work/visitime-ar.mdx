The tour combined maps, narration, archival imagery, and augmented reality. I programmed the Unity experience, geocoded stops across the battlefield, and worked with collaborators to convert historical maps into layers the app could display.

Visitors could rent iPads with the tour installed. I managed the rental fleet and tested the experience on the battlefield, where tree cover and nearby metal could affect location and compass readings.

Screen glare during field testing also led to a separate project. I’m a co-inventor on two U.S. utility patents for a folding shade case for portable devices, [US 9,417,668](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9417668B2/en) and [US 9,900,042](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9900042B2/en).
