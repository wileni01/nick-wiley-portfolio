## Working out what happens at startup

I used Ghidra to reverse-engineer the factory QNX binary and follow what loads before the controls come up. That gave me a place to look for ways to shorten boot time.

Tracing startup also showed me which factory files I wanted to keep. I used Codex to add tests for the read-only backup and file comparison tools using simulated inputs. I checked the files I copied from the car separately and verified that the backup matched.

## Putting it in the car

Once I had my own software running on the QNX unit in the car, I wanted to do something fun with startup. The portrait display plays a short animation, shows a photo of Chief, then brings up the driving controls.
