## Installing the software

I installed the new operating system on the car’s infotainment unit and gave its portrait screen a custom start-up sequence. A short animation leads into Chief’s photo.

## Preserving the factory software

I documented the original setup and copied six factory files from the car to my laptop. I read each file twice and checked that the copies matched byte for byte. I also built an offline interface prototype and tested a read-only backup package with simulated inputs. A full backup and tested restore are still open.
